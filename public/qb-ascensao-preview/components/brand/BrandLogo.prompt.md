Quebrando a Banca brand lockups — use `horizontal` in headers, `wordmark` on ceremonial titles, `vertical`/`app-icon` for splash and stores, `mark` where space is tight.

```jsx
<BrandLogo variant="horizontal" size={44} />
<BrandLogo variant="wordmark" size={40} showMotto align="center" />
<BrandLogo variant="vertical" size={280} />
<BrandLogo variant="app-icon" size={96} />
<BrandLogo variant="mark" mark="sword" size={32} />
```

- Image variants come from the owner's brand sheet (background removed); never recolor, redraw or rotate them.
- Only on dark grounds (navy-deep, obsidian, or a protected scene).
