## Source inventory
The inventory is derived verbatim from the versioned files in this repository, not an independent validation of the primary material. Each entry references SHA-256, source path and original locator. Editorial status in the input is retained but does not approve NEXO relations. Reserved or incomplete activities are excluded. RF question evidence is copied from the existing textoFonte field, not represented as a verified quote from the original book. No student history or database accessed. No new facts, graph edges or approved concepts generated.

## Review gate
Retrieve the underlying source (original legislation, jurisprudence or study material), record exact revision and locator, check the excerpt and proposition, verify both concept IDs in the same scope, validate direction and relation type, then require explicit curator approval for the exact source version before enabling a relation. If the source cannot be opened, remain candidate-only. Never infer is_a or depends_on from tree placement, or contrast from similar words. Existing crosslinks remain literal and untyped until review. No PACIFIC work. No merge or deploy.
